Overview

Robust manipulation needs more than vision — especially when the object is occluded by the hand. This project fuses vision, tactile, and force/torque sensing into one control loop and estimates where contact happens from F/T signals alone.

What I did

  • Built a multi-process orchestration framework integrating vision, tactile (DIGIT, F/T), and control streams with state buffering.
  • Reached 53% success on the Relocate task at RoboWorld 2024.
  • Developed an F/T-based contact-location estimation algorithm with 0.85 mm average error, enabling manipulation under visual occlusion.
  • Presented the estimation method as a poster at ICCAS 2024.
