Overview

A held object is dropped not because the grasp force was wrong but because the arm loses the pose it was commanded: an external push produces torque the model does not account for, and a fixed-gain controller can only answer it after the error has already grown. This adds a Radial Basis Function Neural Network between the trajectory controller and the joint torque command, estimating that residual torque online and cancelling it — so the disturbance is compensated rather than out-muscled by gain.

Why online

The disturbances are not known in advance, so there is nothing to pretrain against. The network's weights update while the arm is moving, from the tracking error it is producing right then; nothing is collected, retrained offline and redeployed. Tested on an xArm7 with an Allegro Hand: without compensation the grasp gave way every time, and with it the object was never dropped. Trial counts were not logged, so that is an observed outcome rather than a measured rate — the tracking plots below are the quantitative part.
