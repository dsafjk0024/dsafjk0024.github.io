Overview

High-payload grasping is fragile to unmodeled dynamics and external disturbances. This project adds a learning-based compensation layer that estimates and cancels residual torque in real time, so the robot keeps its grip even under unexpected interference.

What I did

  • Designed and implemented a full-stack controller using a Radial Basis Function Neural Network (RBFNN) to compensate residual torque.
  • Adopted an online-learning architecture so the controller adapts immediately to unpredictable external forces.
  • Eliminated 100% of object-drop failures in disturbance tests.
  • Presented the work as a poster at KRoC 2026 (Korea Robotics Society Annual Conference).
