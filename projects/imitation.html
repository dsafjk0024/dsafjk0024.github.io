Overview

A team project on evaluation methods for dexterous robot manipulation and grasping. The Peg-in-Hole framework connects teleoperated demonstrations to policy training and real-robot insertion, and it progressed from position-controlled insertion shown at AutomationWorld 2026 to a policy that also observes contact force.

Work on the framework

  • Teleoperation. Robot-side control code for the xArm6 + Robotiq Hand-E driven by a SpaceMouse 6-DoF input device, so an operator could demonstrate the task by hand instead of scripting trajectories — twist input mapped to end-effector motion with the gripper on the same loop.
  • Demonstration collection. Synchronized arm state, gripper state and RealSense frames streamed as one demonstration record — a demonstration is only usable if the three arrive aligned.
  • Policy training and rollouts. Diffusion Policy and flow-matching policies trained on that data and executed on the real xArm6.
  • Live demonstration. The framework ran at AutomationWorld 2026 in March 2026.

This is a team project; the work above was carried out within the team rather than independently. Repeated-trial insertion success rates are not published here.

AutomationWorld 2026 and insertion rollouts

XELA tactile observations for flow matching

XELA sensors on the gripper fingers measure the distribution of contact forces. These measurements were added to the flow-matching policy’s observations during imitation learning. This is a learned tactile-observation extension, not a separate explicit force-control or admittance loop.

The reason is clearance, not occlusion: on these connectors the insertion tolerance is smaller than the position error the controller settles at, so alignment alone does not seat the part. Contact force tells the policy which way the peg is being pushed, and the recordings show the distribution moving across the fingertips as insertion proceeds.

The videos demonstrate execution. A controlled tactile/no-tactile comparison of success rate and insertion time has not yet been aggregated.
