Imitation Framework for Autonomous Robot Manipulation

KETI · 2026 – Present · Contributor (30%) — teleoperation stack & data collection

The task board: USB, heavy-duty, D-sub and RJ45 connectors plus a gear set, each with its own insertion tolerance — the xArm6's Robotiq Hand-E enters from the bottom right. Demonstrations were recorded by hand through a SpaceMouse.

Overview

A team framework for learning precision insertion from human demonstration — collection, training and evaluation on a real xArm6. The task board holds connectors with different insertion tolerances, so success depends on sub-millimetre placement rather than on reaching the right area. I built the teleoperation and data-collection side; a teammate owned the learning side.

My contribution — teleoperation & data collection

  • Wrote the robot-side control code: xArm6 + Robotiq Hand-E gripper driven by a SpaceMouse 6-DoF input device, so an operator could demonstrate the task by hand instead of scripting trajectories.
  • Mapped SpaceMouse twist input to end-effector motion with the gripper on the same loop, and streamed synchronized arm state, gripper state and RealSense frames as the demonstration record — a demonstration is only usable if those three arrive aligned.
  • Operated the rig to collect part of the demonstration dataset the policies trained on.

Teammate's contribution — learning & training

  • Set up the imitation learning environment and implemented the generative policies — Diffusion Policy and Flow Matching — in JAX/Flax.
  • Ran policy training and deployed the trained policies on the real robot for Peg-in-Hole precision insertion.

Team outcome

Within the demonstrated range the insertion succeeded consistently, including during the live demonstration at AutomationWorld 2026; performance fell off once the parts were placed outside it. The team also evaluated the framework on seven NIST-derived metrics — success rate, out-of-distribution adaptability, real-time performance and others — but those results are internal and not published here.
